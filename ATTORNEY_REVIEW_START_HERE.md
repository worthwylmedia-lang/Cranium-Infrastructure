## 2. What the system is

Convertible Cranium is an authority-bound governance architecture intended to separate cognition from canonical authority.

Its current documented authority path is:

1. **Listener:** untrusted ingress.
2. **Engine 1, Synapse:** proposal/context processing, evidence binding, and assessment.
3. **Engine 2A, Substrate A:** constitutional admissibility, asking “May We?”
4. **Engine 2B, Substrate B:** evidence grounding, asking “Is It So?”
5. **Engine 3, Cranium Kernel:** canonical convergence and the sole authority transition boundary.
6. **Governed execution:** execution follows the authorized transition, with receipts, proof, and lineage.
7. **Cross-cutting controls:** Miracle Memory provides governed continuity; Circuit Breaker / COMA supports containment and recovery; Commander OS is an operational surface, not the authority source.

The architecture's central principle is: **cognition may come from anywhere; authority comes only through Convertible Cranium.** Technical novelty, legal novelty, and patentability remain questions for counsel and any necessary prior-art analysis.

## 6. Evidence and claim discipline

The binder distinguishes project-recorded verification from independent verification and from pending validation. It does not claim invulnerability, HSM/KMS-backed custody, independent security certification, completed ChromiumOS production release, market validation, or established patentability.
