# Canonical Authority Invariant

## Formal Invariant

No combination of cognitive output, evidence, memory, UI state, runtime state, agent consensus, external attestation, or any other non-authoritative signal may synthesize, infer, inherit, or substitute for canonical authority.

Canonical authority exists only when the Convertible Cranium Kernel issues a valid governed authority transition under the applicable law, capability, scope, evidence, lifecycle, and integrity requirements.

**Authority cannot emerge. It must be issued.**

## Scope

The invariant applies across cognition, evidence, continuity, execution, runtime, constitutional constraints, attestation, single-agent and multi-agent operation, and external tool or operating-system surfaces.

No plane may elevate itself into authority.

## Consequences

- Cognitive output cannot authorize.
- Evidence cannot authorize.
- Memory cannot authorize.
- UI state cannot authorize.
- Runtime state cannot authorize.
- Agent consensus cannot authorize.
- External attestation cannot authorize.
- Replay cannot authorize.
- Tampered signals cannot authorize.
- Stale sessions cannot inherit authority.
- Combined non-authoritative signals cannot bypass the Kernel.

Authority is non-compositional outside the canonical authority boundary.

## Testable Form

The invariant is a verification property, not merely a design statement. The verification corpus should establish that each prohibited path returns denial, quarantine, containment, or another explicitly governed non-authorizing outcome rather than execution.

Core tests include AI self-approval, Synapse authorization, memory-derived authorization, UI-derived authorization, multi-agent consensus bypass, replayed receipt use, evidence tampering, compromised runtime state, stale-session inheritance, and cross-plane signal composition.

## Corpus Integration

This invariant is the connective architectural rule for the category definition, research paper, architecture, authority model, authority flow, threat model, multi-vector analysis, developer mental model, governed transition example, verification record, IP boundary, and provenance.

Each document may explain a different plane or use case, but none may weaken this invariant.

## Evidence Boundary

The invariant is supported by the verified Kernel revision and its recorded denial, replay, attestation, lifecycle, recovery, and constitutional checks. Coverage of future implementations and the Chromium Edition remains subject to their own executable verification.
