# 38 — Human Utility Roadmap

## Purpose

Convertible Cranium becomes more valuable when people can use the substrate without having to understand the substrate.

The durable product idea is a governed action layer for increasingly capable AI: cognition can propose, but consequential authority remains explicit, bounded, inspectable, reversible, and attributable.

## Five human-value jobs

1. Protect agency: make AI actions require the right authority instead of silently turning confidence into permission.

2. Preserve continuity: give people useful memory with provenance, contradiction handling, quarantine, retention controls, and a deliberate right to forget.

3. Explain consequences: turn every consequential transition into a readable receipt showing what was requested, what evidence was considered, what authority existed, and what actually executed.

4. Recover safely: provide a universal stop, rollback, containment, and restart path when an AI session becomes unsafe or confused.

5. Reduce lock-in: keep the governance layer independent from any single model vendor, browser, operating system, or cloud.

## Product sequence

Phase 1: personal AI safety layer. Govern tool use, external actions, secrets, files, communications, and memory.

Phase 2: Chromium Edition. Make governed AI part of an everyday browser/OS rather than another dashboard people must remember to open.

Phase 3: SDK. Let developers add Kernel-governed capabilities to existing agents without rebuilding the substrate.

Phase 4: portable trust. Standardize receipts, authority envelopes, attestations, capability scope, and recovery semantics across vendors and devices.

Phase 5: human control plane. Give people one understandable place to inspect, stop, recover, revoke, forget, and understand AI actions.

## Design test

Every new feature should answer: “What human problem does this prevent, simplify, or make recoverable?”

A feature that increases model cleverness but does not improve agency, continuity, transparency, recovery, privacy, or interoperability is secondary to the substrate.

## Boundary

This roadmap is a proposed product direction, not a market-validated demand claim. Market validation, usability testing, accessibility research, and independent security review remain future work.

## Current architecture baseline

This document is part of the current Convertible Cranium documentation set. The canonical architecture is Dual-Substrate / Quad-Engine: Cranium AI, Synapse, Governance Review Juror One, and Governance Review Juror Two. The jurors have deliberately different review mandates and processes and neither issues authority.

Commander OS is the operational control surface. Cranium Listener is untrusted ingress. Miracle Memory provides governed continuity. Circuit Breaker / COMA provides cross-cutting runtime containment and recovery. The Convertible Cranium Kernel remains the sole canonical authority source.

The former Eight-Plane and Dual-Engine descriptions are historical framing only and must not be read as the current governance model.
